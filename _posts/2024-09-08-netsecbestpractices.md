Why is network security important?
From the recent report by IBM we can find that The global average cost of a data breach in 2023 was USD 4.45 million, a 15% increase over 3 years.
This figure is certainly a huge financial risk to an organization if it ever happens.
Further we can find that Microsoft mitigates 1,700 DDoS attacks per day according to their report.
Because of this we need to proactively defend against such threats.
Reasons why Network Security is important are:
1. Maintaining Data Integrity and Confidentiality
2. Prevention of unauthorized access
3. Protection against cyber threats
4. Compliance with regulations
5. Business continuity
6. Safeguarding Confidential information

3. Network Segmentation:
	1. Divide your network into smaller logical zones. this will restricts the potential impact of a security breach. Critical systems are isolated from less sensitive areas, like guest networks or public Wi-Fi access points.
	2. To achieve this we can use Firewall, Router and Switch
	3. Seperate logical zones can be created for:
		1. Departments
		2. Functions
		3. Authorization levels
3. Patch Management:
	1. Regularly update Software and Operating Systems to address vulnerabilities.
	2. Automate updates and prioritize business critical systems
4. Least Privilege Access Control:
	1. Assign least privelage to users to complete their job
	2. You can acheive this by:
		1. Restricting user permissions based on their roles and responsibilities
		2. Strong Passwords & Multi-Factor Authentication.
5. Network Monitoring 
	1. Continuously monitor network activity for suspicious behavior that might indicate a potential attack. Tools like SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) systems can help aggregate and analyze log data from various network and endpoint devices
